# Full texts — original pieces, original language

One file per piece at `swipe/<formato>/full-text/<slug>.md`, next to its entry (`swipe/<formato>/<slug>.md`). The complete original text, in its original language, untranslated.

## Language rules (the whole point of these files)

1. **Structure crosses languages, phrasing never.** You may read any full text, in any phase, to study how an argument flows, how a section breathes, how proportions work. What you may NOT do is carry its phrasing into a deliverable written in a different language — no lifted sentences, no translated idioms, no syntax calques.
2. **At drafting time, the working material is the piece plan + skeleton + brief** — not this folder. If you consulted a full text during planning, the register of the deliverable still comes exclusively from `brands/<brand>/brand-copy-rules.md` + [writing-principles](core/writing/writing-principles.md) in the TARGET language.
3. **QA catches what discipline misses**: when consulted swipe language ≠ deliverable language, the writing-principles Fase 4 calque scan applies (anglicisms, source-language syntax carryover, "Not X. But Y." constructions from US swipe).
4. Same-language consultation (IT swipe → IT piece) has no firewall — there, register imitation is the benefit, not the bug.

## Conventions

- Keep the text as-is: typos, formatting quirks and all — it's evidence, not a template.
- Header: 3 lines max (title, source, date) + link back to the entry.
- These files are NEVER read in bulk. One at a time, on purpose.
